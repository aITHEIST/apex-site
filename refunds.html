Limited Refund Eligibility

Refunds may be considered only in the following circumstances:

  • Technical failure: You did not receive the files after purchase and our support team was unable to resolve the delivery issue within 48 hours.

Refunds are not available for:

  • Change of mind after purchase

Required Documentation

All refund requests must include the following. Incomplete requests will not be processed.

  • Your order number (from your Lemon Squeezy receipt email)

We reserve the right to request additional information before making a decision on any refund request.

Refund Process

Eligible refund requests are reviewed within 5–7 business days. Approved refunds are processed through Lemon Squeezy to your original payment method. Bank processing times may add an additional 5–10 business days.

All refund decisions are final and made at our sole discretion.

Customers who initiate fraudulent chargebacks will be permanently blocked from all future purchases and may be reported to fraud prevention databases.
